01 / OVERVIEW

Reliable constraints.
Coherent field maps.

Agricultural scenes challenge visual mapping: crop rows repeat, texture is weak, and vegetation moves in the wind. AgroMap3D estimates which visual constraints to trust while retaining the geometry of the plants.

Abstract −

Dense 3D mapping from agricultural UAV video is difficult due to repetitive crop structure, weak texture, thin vegetation, and wind-driven motion, which can make visual constraints ambiguous or temporally inconsistent. We present AgroMap3D, a reliability-aware framework that uses feed-forward 3D geometry as a local provider and reconciles relative constraints through pose-graph optimization. A dual-rate reliability mechanism evaluates stability on keyframe pairs and a denser temporal stream, down-weighting unreliable pose constraints while retaining vegetation geometry. When available, an uncertain global pose cue provides metric and field-scale context, and the refined poses assemble the local geometry into a dense map. We evaluate AgroMap3D on 22 agricultural UAV sequences under a controlled degraded-pose-cue protocol. The global cue alone achieves 0.300 m mean ATE; adding local geometry reduces it to 0.254 m, while the proposed reliability further reduces it to 0.219 m and improves ATE on all 22 sequences. Across the same progression, RPE-T decreases from 0.258 m to 0.215 m and 0.204 m, while mean rotation error decreases from 6.2° to 5.3° and 5.0°. These results demonstrate complementary gains from field-scale anchoring, local visual refinement, and reliability-aware constraint weighting. Code, evaluation sequences, and trajectory information will be released to support reproducibility.

02 / THE APPROACH

Local geometry.
A field-scale perspective.

A modular pipeline that brings learned geometry, temporal reliability, and uncertain pose cues into one mapping framework.

01

Recover local geometry

A feed-forward 3D model provides dense local pointmaps and relative camera constraints from UAV keyframes.

02

Measure reliability

Keyframe pairs and a denser temporal stream identify unstable evidence and reduce its influence on pose estimation.

03

Refine and assemble

Pose-graph optimization combines the weighted constraints with an optional global pose cue to assemble the dense map.
